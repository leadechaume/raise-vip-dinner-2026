Sponsor Tables

Each contract = Delegation + Fill-in.
Delegation = people the sponsor brings themselves. Covers (a) their own employees (e.g. for IREN: IREN sales, IREN team) and (b) external people they invite directly (not from their org but invited by the sponsor). Both count as "the sponsor filled their seats themselves".
Fill-in = seats CoE places for them, typically with people already in our lists (Free speakers, CxO, VIP MAX, etc.). These people also count in their own quotas elsewhere.
Currently the "Effective guests from the delegation" column covers both (a) and (b) together. When Pauline adds a separate column for "external invitees by the sponsor", we'll show the sub-split here.
